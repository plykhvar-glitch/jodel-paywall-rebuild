# Growth Audit — Jodel (Jodel Plus paywall)

**Live:** https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/jodel-paywall-rebuild/
**Paywall on a phone:** https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/jodel-paywall-rebuild/?view=proto

Jodel Plus opens on “Daily DMs, Never Interrupted by Ads”, thirteen pills of equal weight and 1 Month ticked. This audit rebuilds that one screen so it opens on the feature that brought each Jodler there, shows what changes for them, and says what 1 Year saves, in Jodel’s own design and at Jodel’s own prices. Three phones show three ways in: a chat out of message requests, a full-screen ad, and the Boost button. Under each phone you can switch what opened the paywall.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Open Plus on the feature they tapped | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Put your 4.4 and a real review under the plans | CR +10–15% |
| 03 | Tick 1 Year and tag what it saves | ARPU +10–15% |
| 04 | Show what Plus changes, right under the headline | CR +5–10% |
| 05 | Sort the other twelve pills by when you get them | CR +3–8% |

Closing the paywall goes straight back to where the Jodler was. There is no second paywall.

**Grounding.** The paywall, its copy, pills and prices (8,99 € a week, 12,99 € a month, 74,99 € a year) from the Jodel Plus paywall on iOS in the EU; the same prices and the 10-minute Boost packs are in the German App Store in-app purchase list, 20 November 2026. 4.4 from 74K ratings and the reviews quoted from the App Store. No live ads were found in the Meta Ad Library or the Google Ads Transparency Center on 20 November 2026. 84% is 1,43 € against 8,99 € a week; 52% is 74,99 € against 12 × 12,99 € = 155,88 €. Posts, chats and cities in the prototype are samples.

Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for Jodel.

Run locally: `python3 -m http.server` in this folder, then open http://localhost:8000.
